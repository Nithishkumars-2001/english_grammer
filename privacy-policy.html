Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: October 2026

NM Spoken English Class ("we", "our", "the website") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is used when you visit and use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name or email while you practise. If you contact us through the Contact page, we receive only the details you choose to send.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

To save your score, streak and answered questions, the website uses your browser's local storage. This data stays on your own device and is not sent to our servers. You can remove it any time by using the reset option on the practice page or by clearing your browser data.

3. Cookies And Advertising

Our website shows advertisements provided by third-party services such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalisation through your Google Ads Settings or your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We load fonts and icons from third-party providers (such as Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs). These providers may receive your IP address and browser details when delivering these files.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is meant for general learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please visit our Contact page.
