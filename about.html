About Us

Helping Tamil speakers speak English with confidence.

Namma Story

NM Spoken English Class is a free practice website for Tamil speakers who want to speak English with confidence. Every question shows a Tamil sentence, and you type its English translation. You get instant feedback so you learn from each attempt.

Namma Mission

English kathukkurathu romba kashtam illa — daily konjam practice panna podhum. Our goal is to make spoken English simple, friendly and available to everyone, whether you are a student, a job seeker or a homemaker.

How It Works

  • Choose a level: Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • Read the Tamil sentence and type the English translation within the 60-second timer.
  • Get instant feedback with the correct answer, grammar explanation, word meanings, Tamil meaning and a memory tip.
  • Build your streak and track your score as you improve.

Learn From Mistakes

Every answer comes with a clear grammar explanation and tips.
